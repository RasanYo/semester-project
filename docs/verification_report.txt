10. WHAT THIS DATASET CANNOT DO
==============================================================================
   * The French side of Swissdox is one publisher group (TX Group)
     plus Le Temps and rts.ch. Le Nouvelliste, La Liberte, ArcInfo,
     Le Courrier and Le Quotidien Jurassien are absent from the
     corpus. Language and ownership are collinear by construction.
   * No news agency is in the corpus, so wire copy appears under
     several medium codes. Near-duplicate detection is required
     before any co-positioning claim.
   * Italian Switzerland is rsi.ch alone.
   * Block E is diagnostic and never enters the calibration set.
   * Block C is 'non-institutional', not 'unplanned': Wikidata knows
     only two genuinely unplanned Swiss events in the whole window.
